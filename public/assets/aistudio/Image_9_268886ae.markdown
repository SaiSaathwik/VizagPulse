## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from Apple Pro hardware editorial design: precision-machined, austere, hyper-refined, and quietly authoritative. Tailored for an urban infrastructure, civic accountability, and civic issue resolution platform, the interface replaces traditional municipal bureaucracy with the surgical clarity of a high-end hardware diagnostic tool.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Zero-Gloss Matte Precision:** Rejecting heavy blurs, glossy skeumorphism, and generic glassmorphism in favor of deep matte obsidian fields, deliberate structural contrast, and ultra-crisp micro-geometry.
- **Architectural Bento Grids:** Information is surfaced via engineered modules that compartmentalize complex civic updates, telemetry, incident photography, and geospatial reporting into dense yet breathing units.
- **Utilitarian Elegance:** High-contrast typography paired with tightly controlled status signals creates an experience that feels mission-critical, premium, and calm under operational pressure.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure matte darkness, balanced with high-grade optical neutrals and targeted functional accents.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** Obsidian `#090D12` forms the void background, absorbing light without muddy brown or blue over-saturation.
- **Bento Card Surface:** Steel-Slate `#131822` provides an elevated platform for interactive content, visually separated solely by tonal shift and fine hairline borders.
- **Elevated Interactive Surface:** Raised Slate `#1B2230` for popovers, dropdown sheets, active drawer elements, and segment toggles.

### Structural Hairlines
- **Subtle Perimeter:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` for cards, bento cells, and structural dividers.
- **Active / Focused Hairline:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)` for form inputs, active selection outlines, and focused states.

### Status & Accents
- **Maritime Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Reserved for unassigned reports, urgent citizen escalations, active roadwork, electrical failures, and attention-required telemetry.
- **Coastal Teal (`#0D9488`):** Reserved for validated resolutions, municipal sign-offs, restored services, and positive metrics.
- **Chalk White (`#F9FAFB`):** Primary display text, numerical data points, and active icon strokes.
- **Coastal Slate Mist (`#94A3B8`):** Secondary metadata, timestamps, input placeholders, category tags, and structural subheadings.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes dense, modern-editorial legibility using `Inter` configured to mirror the proportions and optical weight of Apple’s native display interfaces. 

- **Negative Tracking on Display Elements:** Headlines, metrics, and hero sections execute negative letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.035em`) to create a condensed, deliberate, high-impact presence.
- **Architectural Micro-Labels:** Small metadata classifications and telemetry tags utilize `label-caps` set in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic aerospace and hardware laser-etched labelling.
- **Tabular Numerics:** Incident counts, coordinates, SLA countdowns, and civic department IDs leverage tabular lining figures (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`) to preserve alignment across fluctuating real-time figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous matte bento grid system calibrated for high density and rapid scanning on mobile viewports while effortlessly expanding on wider tablet and desktop displays.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px):** Single-column stack or 2-column micro-bento matrix with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) lateral canvas margins.
- **Tablet / Desktop Viewport (>= 640px):** 4 to 12-column modular grid using `1rem` (16px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Cards span dynamically (e.g., 2x1, 2x2, 4x2) depending on information priority.

### Internal Spacing Cadence
- Compact components (tags, micro-buttons, status pills) use `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
- Bento card internal padding strictly adheres to `space-md` (16px) on mobile and `space-lg` (24px) on wider displays.
- Visual separation between unrelated content groupings enforces `space-xl` (40px) to maintain editorial rhythm without relying on colored visual dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated entirely through planar surface tone and ultra-sharp perimeter definition, completely eschewing diffused dropped shadows, neon glows, and glassmorphic blurs.

### Surface Stratification
1. **Level 0 (Environment Canvas):** Pure Matte Obsidian (`#090D12`).
2. **Level 1 (Bento Foundation):** Steel-Slate (`#131822`) with a single crisp 1px hairline border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Level 2 (In-Card Blocks & Embedded Containers):** Darkened Void wells (`#090D12` inset) or Elevated Slate tiles (`#1B2230`) framed with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
4. **Level 3 (Floating Overlays & Modals):** Elevated Slate (`#1B2230`) bordered by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` with a crisp, opaque black perimeter rim (`0 0 0 1px #000000`) for surgical detachment from the canvas.

### Hairline Engineering
Dividers within cards do not use standard solid grey lines; they employ 1px borders rendered at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Under no circumstances should soft drop shadows or ambient colored glows bleed across modules.

## Shapes

The geometric signature balances expansive, structural card curves with hyper-disciplined interior sub-elements.

- **Bento Modules & Outer Shells:** Employ refined radii between `18px` and `24px` (`rounded-xl` equivalent), establishing a warm, hardware-like silhouette reminiscent of milled anodized aluminum casings.
- **Nested Inner Modules & Inputs:** Inner media containers, map snippets, and form fields scale down to `10px`–`12px` to honor proper concentric rounding relationships.
- **Status Badges & Micro Action Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create geometric contrast against the architectural rectangular bento cards.

## Components

### Bento Cards
- **Structure:** Solid `#131822` background, 20px corner radius, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Header:** Upper row integrates an uppercase micro-label (`label-caps` in `#94A3B8`) accompanied by an optional status dot or action icon.
- **Content:** Uncluttered typography hierarchy with `#F9FAFB` for core headlines and `#94A3B8` for secondary notes. Inset images or map previews must clip cleanly to an internal radius of 12px.

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid Chalk White (`#F9FAFB`) fill with pure Obsidian (`#090D12`) typography, semi-bold weight, 12px border radius. Hover/active shifts opacity to `0.92`.
- **Secondary / Action Surface:** Dark Steel-Slate (`#1B2230`) with a 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, Chalk White label. Active state initiates subtle border brightening to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24)`.
- **Accent Primary (Emergency / Report):** Maritime Amber (`#F59E0B`) fill with deep Obsidian text for urgent actions.
- **Ghost Action:** Borderless, Chalk White or Coastal Slate Mist text with immediate background tint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` on interaction.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Urgent / In-Progress:** Pill badge featuring a solid background of `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, 1px hairline border of `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.30)`, and typography in Maritime Amber (`#F59E0B`). Contains a 6px static circle dot indicator.
- **Resolved / Clean:** Pill badge with `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.12)` background, `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.30)` hairline, and Coastal Teal (`#0D9488`) typography.
- **Neutral / Informational:** Pill badge with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` background, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` border, and Slate Mist (`#94A3B8`) text.

### Form Inputs & Geospatial Search
- **Container:** `#090D12` inset canvas or `#131822` surface with a 1px hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, 12px radius, and 14px padding.
- **Typography:** Active value in `#F9FAFB`, placeholder in `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** 1px hairline border crisply highlights to `#F9FAFB` without fuzzy outer focus rings.

### Selection Controls (Checkbox & Radio)
- **Checkbox:** Square box with 4px corner radius, `#131822` background, 1px hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`. Checked state displays a Chalk White checkmark against a solid Maritime Amber or Coastal Teal background depending on functional context.
- **Radio:** Concentric circle with matching hairline; active state shows a centered, unblurred 6px Chalk White dot.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Row Architecture:** Edge-to-edge transparent rows separated by 1px hairline lines `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Metadata Alignment:** Timestamps and location tags positioned flush right, rendered in `label-md` with Slate Mist coloration.
